Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 | Effective Immediately

This Privacy Policy describes how Plestar Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use the ADVT mobile application (the "App") and associated services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide local business discovery and advertisement services, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address, phone number, and full name for authentication and OTP verification.
  • Location Data (Precise and Coarse): With your explicit permission, we collect device location (GPS and network-based) strictly to display nearby local businesses, local job listings, and radius-based promotional offers. Location data is only accessed while using the App.
  • Photos and Media: When business owners create or update a business profile or post advertisements, the App utilizes the system photo picker to select business logos, product photos, and promotional banners. Media is selected exclusively upon user interaction via the standard system picker and is never accessed in the background.
  • Device Information: We collect non-sensitive diagnostic logs, device identifiers, and app version telemetry for performance optimization, stability, and fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users securely via Email One-Time Password (OTP).
  • To discover, rank, and display relevant local business advertisements based on location.
  • To enable business owners to create, publish, and manage promotional ad campaigns.
  • To process paid promotion duration packages securely via Google Play In-App Billing.
  • To maintain service security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

All in-app paid post promotions (1-day, 2-day, 3-day, 7-day, 15-day, 30-day durations) are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, debit card credentials, or banking passwords on our servers.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose data only under the following circumstances:

  • Public Business Information: Business profiles and advertisement posts published by business owners are visible publicly to nearby users.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Hosting and email infrastructure partners operating under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, regulation, or court order.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS) and least-privilege database access to safeguard your data. Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide services or until you request account deletion.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal account and business profiles at any time. To request account deletion and removal of all associated data, contact us at plestarinc@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
